Common questions

Is the credit analysis really free?

Yes. The analysis is free and you're under no obligation to sign up for anything afterward.

Can you guarantee my score will go up?

No one can honestly guarantee a specific result. What we can do is find inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable items on your report and challenge them, and give you a clear plan to build stronger credit.

How long does it take?

It depends on your credit file. Credit bureaus generally have about 30 days to respond to a dispute, and many clients work with us over several months.

Can I dispute errors myself?

Yes. You have the right to dispute inaccurate information directly with the credit bureaus at no cost. We do the research, paperwork, and follow-up for you so you don't have to.

Will this hurt my credit score?

No. Reviewing your own credit report doesn't affect your score.
